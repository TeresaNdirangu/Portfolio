How I work

Find the problem. Decide and write it down. Ship and check.

01

Identify

I start with evidence: Mixpanel funnels, Google Play console and Appstore reports, Firebase Crashlytics, app reviews by users on stores and social media, customer calls and test results. I want to know who is affected and why.

02

Translate

Requirements, acceptance criteria and trade-offs in plain language, so engineers, partners and I agree on what done means.

03

Work on it

I stay in the build with engineering, validating APIs in Postman, clearing blockers, dependencies and risk in stand-ups and sprint reviews. Partners and users hear about changes from me before they find them.

04

Ship

I set release criteria, run UAT and regression, and make the go or no-go call. After release I read the data again and say plainly whether it worked. If it didn't, I say so.
