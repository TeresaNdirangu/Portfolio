How I think about products

Most people meet a product when it works. I meet it when it breaks. That taught me to ask three questions before anything else: who is affected, what is actually going wrong, and how will we know we fixed it? I use the same questions whether I'm testing a release, reading a funnel, or sitting with a hospital team.
